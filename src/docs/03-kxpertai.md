# Using K-XpertAI
> Ask the AI to build, change and check your project.

K-XpertAI is the AI assistant inside KingxTech. Open it with the sparkle button at the bottom right of any page inside your account.

{{video: PASTE_YOUTUBE_LINK_HERE}}

## What it can do

- Create and edit the files in your project from a plain-language request.
- Explain code and fix errors.
- Search the web for current information, such as how a package works today.
- Check your live published site to see whether it loads and whether files such as scripts and styles are found.

## Tips for good results

- Open the project you want to work on first, so the AI knows where to put files.
- Be specific: "Add a contact form with name, email and message, and a Send button" works better than "make it nicer".
- Ask for one thing at a time, then review the result.
- Review changes before accepting them. You can reject a whole response or undo single files.

## Models and your usage

You can choose between models. Stronger models use more of your allowance. Your usage meter shows how much of your current session and weekly allowance you have used. You can also see it on the [Usage page](/usage). When a meter is full, wait for it to reset or upgrade your plan.

## Rules

You may not use K-XpertAI to build malware, phishing pages, scams or anything else listed in the [Terms of Service](/terms). Requests that look like this are flagged for review, and accounts that keep doing it can be suspended.

## Which AI model is used

You can leave this to KingxTech. Open [Console](/console), go to **AI Lab** and find **Model Router**:

- **Auto-Route:** on Pro and Pro+, K-XpertAI starts with a strong model. As your session or weekly allowance fills up it switches to cheaper models, and finally to a free one, so you can keep working instead of being stopped. The Free plan always uses the free model.
- **Best quality:** keeps the strongest model your plan allows for longer. Your allowance runs out faster.
- **Free models only:** always uses free open-source models and never touches your paid allowance.

If you choose a model yourself in a message, your choice always wins. Your meters on the [Usage page](/usage) show how much of your allowance is left.
