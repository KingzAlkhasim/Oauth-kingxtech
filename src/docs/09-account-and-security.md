## Two-factor sign-in

Go to **Account → Security** and turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. After that, signing in asks for a code from the app as well as your password. This is the best protection against someone taking over your account.

## Password and sessions

Change your password under **Account → Security**. If you forget it, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email. Under **Account → Sessions** you can see where you are signed in and sign out other devices.

## The Terms of Service

When the Terms change, you are asked to read and accept them once before you continue. You can read them at any time on the [Terms page](/terms).

## If your account is suspended

If we suspect misuse of the platform, an account can be suspended. A suspended account sees a page with the reason, can contact support, and can download a zip of all their projects. Published sites of a suspended account are taken offline. If the suspension was a mistake or the problem is fixed, contact support. When an account is restored you are told, and you can read the rules again.

## Download your projects

On the suspended page, "Download my data" prepares one zip containing a zip for each project. Environment variables and secrets are not included. The download link works for one hour.
