# Working with others
> Invite people to a project and choose what they can do.

## Invite someone

Open the project and use the share option to invite a person by email, or create an invite link and send it yourself. The invitation expires after a few days. The person signs in or creates an account and accepts.

## Roles

- **Editor:** can change files, use K-XpertAI on the project, publish, and manage restore points.
- **Viewer:** can look at the project and see its visits, but cannot change anything.

The owner keeps full control, including billing, environment variables, domains and deleting the project.

## Limits

Free accounts can invite up to 2 people per project, and Pro and Pro+ up to 10. Files that collaborators add count toward the owner's [storage limit](/docs/plans-and-limits).

## Transfer a project

The owner can transfer a project to another person. They receive a request on their Projects page and can accept or decline it.
