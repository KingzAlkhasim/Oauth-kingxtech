# Plans and limits
> What each plan includes and how storage, restore points and AI usage work.

{{video: PASTE_YOUTUBE_LINK_HERE}}

## Plans

KingxTech has Free, Pro and Pro+ plans. Pro costs $20 for 30 days and can be paid in naira through Paystack or from your wallet balance. See **Billing** for current prices and to upgrade.

## Storage for project files

- **Free:** 25 MB
- **Pro:** 250 MB
- **Pro+:** 1 GB

- Only your source files count. Published builds and restore points do not.
- When storage is full, you can still edit files to make them smaller and delete files, but you cannot add more. Free up space or upgrade.
- Files in a project shared with you count toward the project owner's plan.

Open the [Usage page](/usage) to see exactly what is using your space.

## Restore points

Free keeps the latest 3, Pro keeps 5 and Pro+ keeps 10 per project.

## AI usage

Each plan has a session allowance and a weekly allowance. Stronger models use more. Your meters on the [Usage page](/usage) show how much you have used and when they reset.

## Team members

Free accounts can invite up to 2 people to a project. Pro and Pro+ can invite up to 10.

## Wallet

Your wallet balance is for compute and storage. Pro can be paid from the wallet using the confirm button on the Billing page. Moving money from the wallet to a plan is one-way.

## Servers
A project server (a Node API that sleeps when idle) is a Pro and Pro+ feature. See [Save data and run a server](/docs/data-and-servers). Saving data from your site needs no plan.
